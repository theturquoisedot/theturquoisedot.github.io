Community Kickoff and Insights from GRIP

The first evening of The Turquoise Dot: Turkish professionals, researchers, and students in deep tech in one room, with a wearable-device field report from Dyson, a MedTech project on dementia, and an open discussion on what the community should become in 2026. All 50 seats were taken.

Recap

What happened

Fifty seats, all taken. The kickoff brought together Turkish minds working in deep tech across Singapore's universities, research institutes, and companies, hosted by SMU Institute of Innovation & Entrepreneurship at SMU Connexion.

A Dyson research engineer shared what a wearable device teaches you when it fails, a PhD candidate put a MedTech project on dementia in front of the room for honest feedback alongside insights from the GRIP programme, and the community set out its plans for 2026: quarterly deep dives, rotating venues, and a bridge between Türkiye and Singapore.

Partners: SMU Institute of Innovation & Entrepreneurship (host), Singapore Turkey Friendship Association, and The Mediterranean Deli Turk.
