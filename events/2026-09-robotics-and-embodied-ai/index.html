Venue host A*STAR
Admission Free, registration required

Good to know

Getting there

Fusionopolis One sits directly above one-north MRT (Circle Line). Take the lift to Level 14, Connexis South Tower. The building has gantry access, so registration is required: register on Luma with your full name, company or affiliation, and mobile number, and we submit these for your entry QR code. Registration closes on Monday 21 September at 12:00 and is subject to host approval.

The third deep dive continues our venue rotation: SMU Connexion, then CREATE Tower, now Fusionopolis. Partners: A*STAR (venue host) and NTU GSA Turkish Cultural Group.
